Drift trike drivetrain

Computation & materials

Predicting stiffness from print geometry

Trained CNN and ANN regression models in Keras to predict the stiffness of FDM-printed gyroid beams directly from their sliced print-layer images or G-code — sourced from 72 physical 3-point bending tests, with PCA cutting the feature set to 117 inputs to fit memory limits.

Reached R² values above 0.8, correlating beam porosity to measured stiffness across varying gyroid patterns.

Composites & fabrication — UBC ThunderBikes

Bodywork for an electric race motorcycle

Co-led aerodynamics and composites for a student electric-motorcycle team: photogrammetry-scanned the original bodywork in RealityCapture and Blender, surface-modelled fairings and a gas tank in SolidWorks, then fabricated the parts in carbon fibre and fibreglass with wet-layup and vacuum infusion.

Cleaner surface finish and lower porosity fed straight into CFD analysis used to redesign the mounting brackets.
